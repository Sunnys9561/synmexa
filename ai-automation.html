04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we automate.

Do I need to know how AI works?

No. Start by explaining the repetitive task and how you currently handle it. We can explore the technical options from there.

Can every business task be automated?

No. Some tasks require judgement, context or human approval. We focus on identifying suitable steps rather than automating everything.

Will automation always require paid tools?

Not necessarily. Tool costs depend on the workflow, usage and integrations required. We should check those costs before choosing an approach.

How do I get started?

Tell us about one repetitive task, how often it happens and what makes it difficult. Avoid sharing passwords or confidential customer information.
